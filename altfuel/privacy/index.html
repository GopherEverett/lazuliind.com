AltFuel Finder Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

AltFuel Finder is made by Lazuli Industries LLC. It helps you find public alternative fuel and EV charging stations and is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app uses

Your location (optional). If you allow location access, AltFuel Finder reads your current location while you are using the app, so it can show stations near you. You can say no and search for a place instead. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → AltFuel Finder.

What you search for. When you search for a city, address or ZIP code, the text is sent to Apple's map service to find its coordinates.

Your fuel choice. The app remembers the last fuel type you picked. This setting stays on your device.

Who receives it

  • National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL). To find stations, the app sends a latitude and longitude (yours, or the place you searched for), the search radius and the fuel type to NREL's Alternative Fuel Stations API. NREL's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Apple. Maps, place search and directions use Apple's MapKit services. Apple's privacy policy applies.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.
  • It does not store your location or searches, on your device or anywhere else.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not sell or share data with anyone other than the services above.

Children

AltFuel Finder is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
